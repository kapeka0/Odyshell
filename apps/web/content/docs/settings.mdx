---
title: Settings
description: Manage Human preferences and Organization identity.
---

## Human profile

Open **Settings** from the account menu to change your name, profile photo, and timezone. `System`
follows the current browser. Other choices use an IANA timezone across dashboard dates while the
Server stores timestamps in UTC.

Authentication methods and email addresses remain managed by Odyshell Identity.

## Organization

Open **Settings** in Organization navigation to view its identity, plan, and current Machine and
Agent limits. Members can read these settings. Owners and Admins can rename the Organization and
regenerate its stable visual identity.

Machine Local Policy is owned by each Client Profile. Session supervision lives in **Sessions** and audit
evidence lives in **Activity**; neither is configured through a hidden logging mode.
