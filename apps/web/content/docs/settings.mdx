---
title: Settings
description: Manage your profile, timezone, Workspace identity and Timeline detail.
---

## Profile

Open **Settings** from the account menu to change your first name, last name, profile photo and
timezone. `System` follows the current browser. Other choices use an IANA timezone across dashboard
dates while the Server continues to store timestamps in UTC.

Authentication methods and email addresses remain managed by the identity provider.

## Workspace

Open **Settings** in Workspace navigation to view its identity, plan and current limits. Members can
read these settings. Administrators can change the Workspace name, regenerate its colored avatar
and choose the Timeline logging level used by new Sessions.

Changing the logging level does not alter existing Sessions or reveal content that was previously
excluded. Event Sink configuration remains independent.
